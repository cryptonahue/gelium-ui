## Gelium adaptation

- Mobile-safe `ui-data-table` or semantic plan cards
- Explicit billing basis (month/year, seats, etc.)
- Real product claims and prices only — never invent commercial numbers
- One primary CTA; comparison is not a layout table for unrelated content

## Reject

Invented prices, inaccessible comparison grids, hero-as-pricing without a plan ladder.
