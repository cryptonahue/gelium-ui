## Original (cited)

Live URL: [vercel.com/pricing](https://vercel.com/pricing) — cited region: **Frequently asked questions**.

The FAQ follows the plan comparison and its closing calls to action. Vercel numbers the questions and reveals each answer on demand; this block map records the region's order without copying its skin or wording.

```text
Frequently asked questions
01  Which Vercel plan is right for me?
    answer with a sales contact link
02  Do you offer custom invoicing?
    answer with a sales contact link
03  What are the limits for each plan?
    answer with limits and documentation links
04–12  usage, upgrades, teams, domains, caching, spend, and credits
closing decision links: Talk to sales · Get started for free
```

The source page uses a numbered, progressive-disclosure FAQ region. The reusable jobs are question scanning, optional answer depth, and a clear route to a decision.

## Keep / adapt

| Kept | Gelium primitive | Rejected | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Numbered question scan | native `details` / `summary` with stable `data-value` | Script-only accordion state | Answers must remain available with JavaScript disabled |
| One answer at a time on demand | `ui-accordion` native execution | Copied numbering art or brand styling | Disclosure behavior is portable; the skin is not |
| Answers near the pricing decision | semantic panel sections | Hidden limits or tooltip-only caveats | Cost and constraint information needs a durable reading path |
| Closing next step | one `ui-button ui-button-primary` link | Several competing CTAs after every answer | The FAQ supports one decision instead of restarting the page |

## Ask before copying

- Which pricing questions are driven by real support volume, and who owns keeping their answers current?
- Which limits, usage calculations, and commercial claims need a canonical source or legal review?
- Should answers open by default for the first visit, or should the initial state stay fully collapsed for scanning?
